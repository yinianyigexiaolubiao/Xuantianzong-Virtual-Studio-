# Promo Film 01 — Seedance Candidate Review

Review basis: five extracted frames per candidate (`start`, `25%`, `50%`, `75%`, `end`). This is an objective first-pass drift screen, not final visual acceptance. A five-frame sheet can expose large shape/composition changes and sampled morphing, but it cannot conclusively clear between-sample flicker.

## Shot 01 — establishing

- `candidate_seedance_01`: pronounced Xuantian Peak silhouette/massing change across the sampled sequence; the distant floating form develops a tall spired crown and enlarged hall cluster. Terrain/peak proportions change materially. Recommend redraw.
- `candidate_seedance_02`: peak and terrain remain more temporally consistent than candidate 01, but the floating peak reads as a broad dune-like mass and loses the locked visual identity. Recommend redraw.
- Sampled issues: `Xuantian Peak drift`, `geometry drift`, `terrain readability`.

## Shot 02 — gate reveal

- `candidate_seedance_01`: gate opening and forward reveal remain spatially continuous in the sampled frames. Sword/eave silhouettes simplify and change as they emerge; the two 44 m V10 straight-sword identities are not consistently readable.
- `candidate_seedance_02`: similar gate continuity; added finial/ornament detail appears on the sword/eave silhouette and changes its reading relative to the graybox anchor.
- Sampled issues: `sword drift`, minor `gate drift`. No sampled camera teleport.

## Shot 03 — gate crossing

- `candidate_seedance_01`: continuous passage through the opening is readable. Distant terrain changes from simple triangular peaks to a different grouped formation after the crossing.
- `candidate_seedance_02`: gate passage remains continuous; distant mountain silhouette becomes a denser jagged ridge at the end.
- Sampled issues: `geometry drift`, `terrain drift`. No sampled wall jump or camera teleport.

## Shot 04 — inner axis

- `candidate_seedance_01`: road/stair/portal sequence remains continuous in the five samples; small vegetation and stair-edge shapes vary.
- `candidate_seedance_02`: closely follows candidate 01; minor stair/portal proportion and vegetation changes are visible.
- Sampled issues: minor `object morphing` in proxy vegetation/stair edges. No large sampled camera or axis drift.

## Shot 05 — Xuantian Peak

- `candidate_seedance_01`: severe scale and silhouette change from the near overhead floating mass to the later side view; crown, underside and platform relationships do not remain a stable locked identity. Recommend redraw.
- `candidate_seedance_02`: more stable camera distance and overall mass than candidate 01, but the main floating body still changes thickness and underside profile across the shot. Recommend redraw before selection.
- Sampled issues: `Xuantian Peak drift`, `geometry drift`, `object morphing`.

## Shot 06 — Xuantian Hall

- `candidate_seedance_01`: hall approach remains spatially continuous; roof edge, column spacing and stair/platform edges show small sampled changes.
- `candidate_seedance_02`: similar continuity with minor roof/platform proportion drift.
- Sampled issues: minor `Xuantian Hall drift`, minor `object morphing`. No sampled camera teleport.

## First-round disposition

- Strongest candidates for further human viewing: Shot 04 candidates 01/02 and Shot 06 candidates 01/02.
- Recommended Seedance redraws: Shot 01 candidates 01/02, Shot 05 candidates 01/02; Shot 02 should be redrawn if exact V10 twin-sword readability is mandatory in this shot.
- Potential Wan repair candidates: Shot 03 may benefit from a short post-gate terrain transition repair; Shot 02 may benefit from a short reveal repair if a Seedance redraw still changes sword/eave silhouettes. Shot 01 and Shot 05 show identity-level drift and should not be treated as short-transition repairs.
- Flicker and texture instability require continuous playback review; the sampled contact sheets do not establish their absence.
